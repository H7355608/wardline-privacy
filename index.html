WARDLINE — Privacy Policy

Effective date: 17 August 2026 · Developer: LAB—47 · Contact: dinon3haroon@gmail.com

WARDLINE is a mobile game published by LAB—47. This policy explains what data the game handles and what it does not.

What the game itself collects

Nothing. WARDLINE has no accounts, no sign-in, and no server of its own.

  • Save data (your hospital, progress, and settings) is stored only on your device.
  • The backup code feature copies your save, encoded, to your device's clipboard. It is never transmitted anywhere by the game; where you paste it is up to you.
  • The game does not access your contacts, location, camera, microphone, photos, or files.

Advertising

WARDLINE shows ads through Google AdMob (the Google Mobile Ads SDK). To serve and measure ads, Google may collect information from your device, including:

  • Advertising ID
  • IP address and coarse, IP-derived location
  • Device and app information (model, OS version, app version)
  • Ad interaction data (views, taps) and diagnostics

This collection is performed by Google under its own policies:

  • How Google uses data from apps that use its services
  • Google Privacy Policy

You can reset or delete your Advertising ID, or opt out of ad personalisation, in your device settings under Google → Ads.

Consent (EEA / UK)

Where required by law, the game shows Google's consent form (User Messaging Platform) before any ad is requested, and the choices you make there govern how ads are served to you. You can be shown non-personalised ads if you decline personalisation.

Rewarded ads

Watching an ad for an in-game reward is always optional and always initiated by you.

Children

WARDLINE is not directed at children under 13, and the store listing's target audience excludes them.

Data retention and deletion

Because the game holds your data only on your device, deleting the app (or using the in-game wipe progress option) removes it. Data collected by Google for advertising is retained under Google's policies linked above.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted at this URL with a new effective date.
